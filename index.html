01 Offline-first storage

Instant by default. Offline by design.

There is no spinner between you and your notes. Vespera writes to a local store the moment you type, indexes it in place, and renders from memory. Flights, tunnels, dead zones — nothing changes.

  • Local write-through. Saves land on device first, always — sync never blocks writing.
  • Zero-latency editing. No network round trip sits in the typing path.
  • Useful before sign-in. Install it, write for a week, connect cloud storage later.
  • Yours to move. Plain markdown underneath, exportable at any time.

02 Google Drive appData sync

Sync through a door only Vespera can open.

Vespera does not scatter your notes across your Drive. It writes one compact ledger — vespera_sync.json — into appDataFolder, the hidden per-app space Google reserves for applications. It never appears in My Drive, and it is not shared with anything else you store.

Hidden appDataFolder

Sync lives in the app-private Drive scope, not in your documents. Files you keep in Drive stay untouched and unlisted.

03 Private vault & encryption

A vault inside your notes.

Mark any note as vault-only and its content is sealed on the device before it is ever written or synced. The key comes from your password through PBKDF2-SHA-256, and the note itself is protected with 256-bit AES-GCM. There is no recovery email, because there is no server holding your key.

Vault ledger notes

Client-side only: the plaintext of this note never leaves the device, not even to Google Drive. If I ever lose the password, the ciphertext stays ciphertext.

    ★ Privacy model

    No tracking. No telemetry. Nothing to opt out of.

    Vespera Notes keeps your writing on hardware you own and syncs it only to storage you already control. There is no analytics SDK in the build, no session recording, and no crash pipeline that carries note content.

    Client-side storage
    Notes and the search index live on the device, encrypted at rest when they are vault notes.
    Direct OAuth tokens
    Your device talks to Google with your own grant. No intermediary server ever sees the payload.
    No account, no profile
    Install and write. There is no Vespera login, no email capture and no device fingerprint.
    No analytics or ads
    Zero tracking libraries ship in the Android or Windows build. Nothing phones home.
